Guidelines

  1. Be respectful

    Do not bully, harass, threaten, or target other students or staff. JPC Loop should feel like a safe space for students to share and interact.

  2. Keep it school appropriate

    Posts, comments, images, and usernames should be suitable for a school website used by Years 7-13. Avoid explicit, offensive, or inappropriate content.

  3. Do not share private information

    Do not post someone's phone number, address, private messages, personal issues, or photos of them without permission.

  4. Keep it relevant to JPC or student life

    Posts should connect to JPC, school events, clubs, sports, student news, questions, announcements, or things students would actually care about.

  5. Use images responsibly

    Only upload images you have permission to use. Avoid copyrighted images, personal-use-only images, or photos of people who have not agreed to be posted.

  6. Respect staff and students

    You can give opinions, jokes, or feedback, but do not use the site to insult teachers, students, year groups, houses, or teams.

  7. Think before posting

    Before posting, consider whether it could embarrass someone, start unnecessary drama, or cause problems at school.
