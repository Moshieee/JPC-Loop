About MacKillop House

MacKillop House is JPC’s green house, named after Saint Mary MacKillop, also known as Saint Mary of the Cross. She co-founded the Sisters of St Joseph of the Sacred Heart, who played a major role in Catholic education and later staffed MacKillop College in Rotorua. MacKillop College was one of the two schools that combined to form John Paul College. Today, MacKillop House continues this connection to faith, education, and community through participation in JPC’s house events and school activities.
