About Kanea House

Kanea House is JPC’s yellow house, named after Mere Kanea. Kanea was connected to important Te Arawa history through her father, Mita Taupopoki, a high-ranking Tūhourangi and Ngāti Wāhiao leader who helped support Catholic life in Rotorua. Mere Kanea is remembered as someone who lived out her Catholic faith and became a role model for young women of Te Arawa. Today, Kanea House represents pride, participation, and school spirit through JPC’s house events, competitions, and activities.
