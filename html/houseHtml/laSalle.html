About La Salle House

La Salle House is JPC’s purple house, named after Saint John Baptist de La Salle. He was a French Catholic saint who dedicated his life to improving education for children and founded the Brothers of the Christian Schools, also known as the De La Salle Brothers. He is recognised as the Patron Saint of Teachers. JPC is a Catholic school in the Lasallian tradition, so La Salle House reflects the school’s focus on faith, learning, service, and community.
