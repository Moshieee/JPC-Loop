About Rice House

Rice House is JPC’s red house, named after Edmund Ignatius Rice, the founder of the Christian Brothers. The Christian Brothers played an important role in Catholic education and helped staff Edmund Rice College, which later became part of John Paul College. Today, Rice House is part of JPC’s house system, where students compete, participate, and earn spirit through sports, events, and school activities. Rice is about representing the house with pride, getting involved, and contributing to the wider JPC community.
